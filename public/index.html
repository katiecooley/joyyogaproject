About the practice

Katonah Yoga® is a canon of techniques that reliably create the conditions for loading patterns that you may not normally inhabit. This gives one’s mind and body a great deal of new sensory information. Muscle, bone, and tissue that usually stabilize through habitual strategy may momentarily decrease control, allowing other tissue to contribute differently. Mentalization, through imagery, narrative, therapeutic metaphor, archetype, and sacred geometry, creates a context that can take one out of the efforts of their personal embodiment and lead to greater integration of mind and body.

People often come to this practice looking for a way to be in their bodies in a way that is more aligned with who they are on the inside. In that way, it is a kind of bringing forth of the implicit.

This isn’t yoga as fitness, although it will build breath capacity, strength, and mobility. And it isn’t yoga as stress relief, although you will be able to hold more complexity and paradox. Instead, it is a practice of learning to fit yourself so you can live and move in the ways germane to who you are and who you are becoming.

On the name. Nevine Michaan, who developed Katonah Yoga®, teaches that when we face the direction of joy, the rearview mirror reveals everything that brought us here. The practice is in that spirit — oriented forward, with what came before integrated and visible behind.
